Hemp-derived THC drinks add a licensing and legal risk that is unresolved. See [](06-licensing-and-legal.md#hemp-drink-licensing) for the licensing detail.

> **Research (unverified):** The FY2026 agriculture appropriations act (Public Law 119-37, Section 781) rewrote the federal definition of hemp to a total-THC test, and finished products with more than 0.4 milligrams of total THC per container are excluded from the definition of hemp. Sources report an effective date of November 12, 2026, with a later law (P.L. 119-103) reported to have delayed at least part of it to December 11, 2026. The sources disagree about which parts moved, so the effective date for drinks is uncertain. Unless Congress acts, hemp THC drinks above that cap would lose federal hemp status in every state.
>
> In Tennessee, hemp-derived cannabinoid products moved to the Tennessee Alcoholic Beverage Commission (TABC) on January 1, 2026, and anyone selling them needs a TABC license. Reported eligibility: a retail hemp license is limited to businesses that also hold a TABC retail package store license or a TABC on-premises consumption license. Spun's current plan is a local beer permit, so eligibility is in doubt. Selling without a license is reported to be a Class A misdemeanor, and TABC reported finding more than 650 unlicensed retailers.
>
> Sources: [Congress.gov CRS: Change to Federal Definition of Hemp](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IN12620), [CRS: Changes to the Statutory Definition of Hemp](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IF13136), [Hemp Law Group: Federal Hemp Ban 2026](https://www.hemplawgroup.com/news/federal-hemp-ban-2026-where-things-stand), [December 11 Hemp THC Ban (hempbeverages.com)](https://hempbeverages.com/pages/hemp-law-2026), [TABC HDCP Retail Applicant Guide](https://www.tn.gov/content/dam/tn/abc-documents/abc-documents/HDCP-Retail-Applicant-Guide.pdf), [Adams and Reese: Tennessee HDC law](https://www.adamsandreese.com/last-call/what-to-know-about-licensing-and-selling-under-tennessees-new-hemp-derived-cannabinoid-law), [TABC: 650 unlicensed retailers](https://www.tn.gov/abc/public-information-and-forms/newsroom/2026/3/13/tabc-investigates-650-unlicensed-hemp-retailers-across-tennessee-.html)
>
> **Verify with:** the enacted text of P.L. 119-37 and P.L. 119-103; TABC licensing office; a hemp or cannabis attorney.
